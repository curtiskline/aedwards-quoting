Trust Ramp

The engine scores every quote and, at Tier 1, recommends high-confidence ones for send. At Tier 2 it auto-sends the safe slice: quotes passing every guardrail (all lines priced, known customer, confirmed ship-to, price in tolerance, allowlisted recipient, above the auto-send threshold, under the dollar ceiling, no holds) go out automatically; everything else falls to a human. Tier 0 is the kill-switch, and dropping from Tier 2 to 0 or 1 stops all auto-sends immediately.
