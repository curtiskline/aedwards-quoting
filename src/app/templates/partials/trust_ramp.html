Trust Ramp

The engine scores every quote and, at Tier 1, recommends high-confidence ones for send. Every send is still clicked by a person. Tier 0 is the kill-switch: recommendations are suspended and everything is fully manual. Auto-send tiers (2-3) arrive in a later stage and will read this same setting.

Send holds

A hold forces every matching quote to Not recommended regardless of its confidence score. Use it to pause a customer or a product type while something is being sorted out. Later auto-send stages must honor these same holds.
